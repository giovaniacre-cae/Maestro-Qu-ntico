## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an exquisite harmony between deep quantum science and high-end holistic wellness. Built for high-precision radiesthesia, vibratory assessment, and harmonic resonance analysis, the visual language merges the rigor of laboratory clinical diagnostics with the sacred warmth of bespoke energetic therapies.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Modern Clinical Meets Sacred Luxury**: Utterly devoid of chaotic or esoteric clutter; instead, it adopts an editorial, clinical precision underpinned by understated opulence.
- **Atmospheric Glow & Resonance**: Subtle gold halos (`rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.08)` to `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`), crystalline dividers, and precise vector geometry communicate frequency alignment, biometer calibration, and energetic coherence.
- **Emotional Resonance**: Dignified, calm, authoritative, and profoundly restorative. The practitioner feels like an orchestrator of subtle fields, backed by absolute analytical fidelity.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative deep midnight blues against solar and earthen golden frequencies, settled onto an immaculate, soft warm-tinted clinical canvas.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0F172A`)**: Midnight Obsidian. Serves as the anchor for supreme contrast, grounding typographic headers, core metrics, and active state controls.
- **Secondary (`#D97706`)**: Resonant Amber. Used for harmonic peaks, active radiesthetic markers, biometer needles, and vibrational attunements.
- **Tertiary (`#B45309`)**: Deep Burnished Gold. Provides rich definition for high-tier telemetry, badges, sacred ratio highlights, and active tab highlights.
- **Subtle Highlight (`#F59E0B`)**: Radiant Solar Corona. Reserved strictly for resonance halos, delicate micro-indicators, and luminous glow accents.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF8F5` / `#F8FAFC`)**: Soft Ice Linen background ensuring effortless reading without stark clinical glare.
- **Card Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure optic white planes framed with whisper-soft contouring borders (`#E2E8F0`).

## Typography

The typographic system creates an intentional dual voice:
1. **Sacred & Editorial (Merriweather)**: Imparts ancestral dignity, classical authority, and holistic luxury to diagnostic session headers, therapeutic conclusions, and biometer indices.
2. **Clinical & Metric Precision (Inter)**: Delivers razor-sharp clarity for numerical data, Bovis scales, electromagnetic hertz calibrations, biofeedback logs, and clinical notation.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined, breathable 12-column grid anchors the desktop workstation, collapsing dynamically into 4 columns on mobile diagnostic handhelds.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column architecture with `gutter` of 1.25rem and `margin` of 2rem. Accommodates split-view analytical consoles: left biometer and pendulum frequency canvas, right therapeutic intervention dossier.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns, tight `gutter-mobile` of 0.75rem, canvas outer `margin-mobile` of 1rem to prioritize visual biometer diameter and live waveform monitors.
- **Spatial Rhythm**: Layout elements favor generous internal breathing room (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to lower cognitive load during delicate radiesthetic and sensory measurement sessions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects exaggerated drop shadows in favor of luminous clinical depth and soft spectral ambient diffusions.

- **Baseline Cards**: Placed on pure `#FFFFFF` over `#FAF8F5`, bounded by a hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and an ambient, low-density shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Harmonic & Active State Elevation**: Resonating cards or actively scanning biometers introduce a layered luminous halo: `0 0 24px 0 rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.08), 0 8px 30px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Overlays & Modals**: Frosted clinical crystal backdrop (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px) bg-white/80`) paired with an authoritative floating shadow `0 20px 40px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The interface balances clinical structure with organic sacred geometry.
- Corner radii are systematically standardized at `roundedness: 2` (0.5rem base radius).
- Radiesthesia dials, chakra alignment wheels, and Bovis arc gauges employ pure circular arcs (`rounded-full`) nested within structured rectangular containment cards.
- Interactive controls and telemetry containers utilize `rounded-lg` (1rem) for an inviting, tactile luxury feel.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Vibrational Attunement)**: Background `#0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`, subtle inset border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. On hover, subtle transition to deep amber glow halo (`0 0 16px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary (Resonant Action)**: Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#B45309`, border `1px solid #D97706`. On hover, fills with delicate wash `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.06)`.

### Radiesthesia Biometers & Gauges

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Normal/Harmonic**: Background `#FAF8F5`, text `#0F172A`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Active Frequency / Resonance**: Background `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.08)`, text `#B45309`, border `1px solid rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.3)`. Text set in uppercase `label-sm`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#D97706`, accented by a gentle 2px gold ring glow (`rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.15)`). Helper text and calibration units set in `body-sm` (`#64748B`).

### Diagnostic Cards
- White clinical base (`#FFFFFF`) with `space-md` to `space-lg` internal padding. Top edge features an optional 2px hairline golden gradient rule (`linear-gradient(90deg, #D97706 0%, transparent 100%)`) denoting active biofield analysis.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Unchecked: 1.5px border `#94A3B8`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked: Background `#0F172A` with golden inner indicator `#F59E0B`, creating a high-contrast jewel effect.
